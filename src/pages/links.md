# Links

Some websites and tools that I like and keep coming back to.

## Tech

Self-hosted, open-source tools that I use in my home lab (see [projects](./projects/)).

### [Nextcloud](https://nextcloud.com/)

Personal cloud storage for files, photos, calendars and contacts.

### [Passbolt](https://www.passbolt.com/)

Open-source password manager, based on OpenPGP. See my [installation guide on Ubuntu 24.04](./projects/passbolt_Ubuntu24.04_Apache).

### [BookStack](https://www.bookstackapp.com/)

Simple wiki to organise and share documentation.

## Low-tech & upcycling

I like everything around low-tech and upcycling: repairing, reusing and building simple, sustainable solutions.

### [Low-tech Lab](https://lowtechlab.org/)

A collaborative project that explores, documents and shares open-source low-tech solutions.

### [L'Obsolèterie](https://www.facebook.com/lobsoleterie/)

Restores and rescues furniture and decorative objects considered obsolete.

### [L'ArchiPelle](https://larchipelle.fr/)

A media about autonomy, permaculture and know-how, with video reports and articles.

## Oenology

I followed several wine introduction courses with [On Veille au Grain](https://onveilleaugrain.fr/) (Romain Cipolla): « S'y connaître en vin », « De la vigne au verre » and « L'Afrique et l'Océanie » (see [the course cycles](https://onveilleaugrain.fr/cycles-oenologie)). They gave me the keys to taste, understand and choose wines from all over the world.
